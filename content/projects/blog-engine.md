## The problem

Every static-site generator I tried felt either too magical or not magical enough. I wanted something I could read end-to-end in one sitting.

## What I did

A few hundred lines of React, three CSS theme files, a hash router, and a JSON-shaped data file. No build step, no plugins, no markdown. The constraints turned out to be the feature.

## What it taught me

If you write your own portfolio engine, you'll never run out of excuses to redesign it. This is both the upside and the curse.
