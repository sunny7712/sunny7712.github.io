## The problem

I wanted to understand GANs deeply enough to debug one. Reading the paper wasn't getting me there.

## What I did

Implemented DCGAN from scratch in PyTorch — generator, discriminator, transposed convs, batch norm, the whole apparatus. Wrote the training loop, hooked up TensorBoard, watched the loss oscillate in real time.

## What it taught me

Half of understanding a paper is rebuilding it. The other half is staring at a confused discriminator at 2 a.m. wondering why it stopped learning.
